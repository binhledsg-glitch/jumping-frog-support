Frequently asked questions

Select a question to read the answer.

How do I jump?

Swipe an empty part of the scene to look around. Tap Aim Your Jump, drag on the frog to adjust direction and angle, then hold Hold to Jump and watch the power meter above the button. Release to jump. You cannot steer once the frog is in the air.

Why did I lose when the frog appears to be on the golden target?

The frog's entire ground-contact footprint must fit inside the target; the visible model alone does not determine the result. Your jump must also pass through the wall's width, rather than around either end. Hitting the wall or a flying obstacle earlier still counts as a failure, even if the frog later reaches the target. If the result looks unexpected, send us the level number and a description of the jump.

Do I have to clear the highest part of the wall?

No. You can pass through a gap or over a lower section, provided you avoid the wall blocks and land fully inside the golden target.

What happens after three failed tries?

You can start a fresh round with three tries. There is no wait or required ad to play again.

What do the items do?
  • Jump Shoes: helps you jump higher.
  • Long Jump: increases jump distance.
  • Aim Goggles: previews the trajectory.
  • Wide Pad: enlarges the target.
  • Second Chance: costs 100 coins per unit; each use consumes one unit and restores one try after a failed jump.

You can select one launch booster at a time. Second Chance is used separately after a failure.

Why did buying an item not activate it?

Buying adds an item to your inventory. Select it before jumping. A launch booster is consumed when you release the jump button, including on a failed jump. Second Chance does not refund a booster used on that jump.

Do I have to collect the gifts on every tenth level?

No. Gifts are optional challenges on levels 10, 20, …, 100, and each gift can be collected once. Reaching one requires the appropriate Jump Shoes or Long Jump booster. You can clear the level without collecting the gift.

How do I change outfits or worlds?

Open Skins and choose Frog or World. A newly purchased appearance is equipped automatically. For something you already own, tap Equip. Coins are game currency; the current version does not sell coins or items for real money.

How many coins does Watch Ad ×2 award?

If you complete the rewarded ad's requirements and the game receives reward confirmation, you receive an additional amount equal to that win's normal coin reward. ×2 applies to the win, not your entire wallet. Closing an ad alone does not guarantee a reward. A win can receive the bonus only once.

Why is an ad unavailable or the button not ready?

Ads require an internet connection and depend on loading, privacy choices and ad availability. You can wait and try again or continue playing. Your normal reward for clearing the level is kept. If you completed an ad but did not receive the bonus, contact support with the level, approximate time and game version if known.

Can I transfer my progress to another device?

Progress is currently stored on your device, with no account or cloud sync. Deleting the app may remove progress. Contact support before reinstalling as a troubleshooting step. We do not have a server copy that guarantees recovery of lost progress.

How do I change audio, text size or language?

Tap the gear icon to adjust music and sound effects. More includes text size, reduced motion and graphics quality.

The game follows a supported language matching your device and falls back to English when needed. Current languages are English, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Spanish, Portuguese (Portugal), French, German, Cantonese (Traditional) and Mandarin (Simplified). Vietnamese is not currently an in-game language.

Where can I read about privacy or change my choices?

Read our Privacy Policy. When the service requires a privacy options entry, the game shows Settings → More → Ad privacy choices. For help or privacy requests, email binh.le2207@gmail.com.

How do I report a problem?

Email binh.le2207@gmail.com with the subject Jumping Frog – Support and these details:

  • iPhone model and iOS version.
  • Game version, if known.
  • Level number and the steps just before the issue.
  • What you expected and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot or video of the in-game situation, if useful.

You can also use this address for feedback and feature suggestions.
