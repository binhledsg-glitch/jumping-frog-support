Privacy Policy

Jumping Frog · Updated October 15, 2026 · Effective upon publication on this website

1. Who we are and what this covers

Ho Kinh Doanh Binh Le develops and operates Jumping Frog. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the iPhone version, visit these support and privacy pages, or contact us for support.

Privacy contact: binh.le2207@gmail.com.

2. Game information stored on your device

The game stores level progress, coins, items, owned and equipped cosmetics, selected worlds, audio settings and interface preferences on your device. Randomly generated profile identifiers, play history and reward records help resume your game and prevent coins or items from being credited or deducted more than once.

The app also keeps local recovery copies and diagnostic information about ad loading and reward processing. Our game code does not automatically upload these local profiles, receipts or diagnostic files to a publisher server.

You do not need an account. The current version does not offer player accounts, publisher-hosted progress storage or a service for syncing progress between devices.

3. Advertising and Google services

The game integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) for banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) helps handle privacy choices.

These services may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from the network, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions such as opening the game, performance information, crash information and diagnostics. Uses include ad delivery and measurement, service operation, privacy choices and fraud prevention. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

The app currently requests nonpersonalized ads. This setting can still involve data processing for ad delivery and measurement. We may receive aggregate advertising reports from AdMob; this does not sync your game profile.

Google describes its processing in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Google and its infrastructure providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

4. Your choices

Where required for your region and the service configuration, the app presents a privacy message before loading ads. You can revisit available choices through Settings → More → Ad privacy choices when that entry appears. Menu labels follow the game's language. If the entry is unavailable, you can email us for guidance on privacy requests.

Watch Ad ×2 is optional. You can continue playing without watching a rewarded ad. Bonus coins are granted only when the advertising service confirms that the reward requirements were met; closing a video early does not automatically earn a reward.

The game does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photo library or precise GPS location to play. Advertising services may still infer approximate location from your IP address.

5. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to provide, such as your iPhone model, iOS version, game version, level and a description of the issue. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests.

Our support mailbox uses Gmail. Email providers process messages to deliver and store them under their own terms and policies. We use support correspondence for these purposes, resolving disputes or meeting applicable legal obligations.

6. Support and privacy websites

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. As explained in the GitHub Pages documentation, GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. The hosting provider's processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not add data collection forms, visitor analytics or tracking cookies to these pages. Selecting an email link uses your own email app or provider to send a message; the website does not automatically send support information.

7. Retention and deletion

Game information stays in the app's storage so you can continue playing. Using iOS Delete App removes the app's local data container from that device. Offload App may keep the data. Backups or restores managed by iOS may still contain information; you manage those through Apple's settings. The game currently has no button to delete all local data and no account-based progress recovery.

We retain support messages for as long as needed to resolve your request, follow up on related problems or meet applicable legal obligations. You can email us to request deletion. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve a dispute.

Information processed by Google is subject to Google's retention policy. Deleting the game does not automatically delete information from Google's systems. For requests within Google's control, we will direct you to the appropriate Google privacy channel.

8. Privacy requests

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. Contact binh.le2207@gmail.com and describe the information or processing involved. We will handle requests under applicable law and may need limited information to verify the requester. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.

We cannot remotely access a game profile stored only on your iPhone. For information independently controlled by a third party, you may also need to use that party's request channels.

9. Children's privacy

Jumping Frog is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child's information has been handled inappropriately through the game or our support channel, contact binh.le2207@gmail.com. We will review the concern and help address it under applicable law. The game does not ask players to enter a name, email address or date of birth to start playing.

10. Policy changes

When features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its update date. Changes requiring consent will be handled as applicable requirements provide. Please email us if you need more information.
